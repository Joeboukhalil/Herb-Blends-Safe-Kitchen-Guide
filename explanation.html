Detailed, general information about culinary herbs. Not medical advice.

What this page is

Pages for each herb include: short description, common active components (kitchen-level), typical culinary uses, safe kitchen-style amounts for a single cup, blending tips, steeping guidance, and cautions. This guide is intended for educational and culinary use only.

Do NOT treat this as medical advice — herbs can interact with medications and may be unsafe for children, pregnant or breastfeeding people, or people with certain chronic conditions. Always check with a doctor or pharmacist first.

Steeping & amounts (single cup)

  • Dried herbs: usually 1–2 tsp per cup as a starting point.
  • Fresh herbs: about 1 tbsp chopped fresh leaves per cup.
  • Green tea: 1 tsp leaves (75–85°C recommended).
  • Lavender & rosemary: use small amounts (pinch to ½ tsp) — strong flavor.
Start milder if you're new to an herb. Taste and adjust.
